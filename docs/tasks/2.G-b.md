# 2.G-b Phase 2 gate fixes, round B

Phase 2 gate findings: the benchmark gate, flaky tests, sandbox-save-check
in CI, missing save benchmarks. Branch `task/gate2-b` from 5b5e534.

## 1. Benchmark gate (must)

**What was wrong.** `save/column_insert` was over its 500 ms budget on the
CI runner on every push from 2.4c onwards, at 712 ms to 1.06 s (run
37290386769: 1.06 s, with 739 ms of that in its no-disk twin). So the
Benchmarks workflow was red the whole time. Because `bench-report` failed
attempt 1 outright on a budget, attempts 2 and 3 never ran either, and no
regression was judged from 2.4c to the gate.

**The ordering (fixed whatever ADR-0015 decides).** `Report::outcome` no
longer returns `Fail` for a budget before it has decided about a rerun:

- An attempt that noise or a regression would rerun still asks for the
  rerun (exit 3), and for the third attempt.
- `Report::after` carries attempt 1's budget failures forward
  (`earlier_over_budget`); the last attempt fails for them. Over budget
  and quiet on attempt 1 still fails at once (nothing to rerun).
- Annotations and the job summary say so; the justfile loop only got
  comments. Tests: `a_budget_failure_does_not_stop_regressions_being_judged`
  and the updated `a_budget_fails_on_either_attempt`.

**Report-only budget (provisional, ADR-0015 proposed).** Rob, 5 October:
first paint and reading are what matter, and a genuinely slow operation
may be slow as long as no obvious performance is left on the table.

- `budgets::REPORT_ONLY = ["save/column_insert"]`: over its limit it is
  `Status::OverReportOnlyBudget`, a warning. Missing still fails, and the
  no-disk twin is still judged at 20%. Remove the id to make it hard.

**One profile, cheap fixes only.** `sample` on `save/column_insert_no_disk`
(M5 Pro): the walk is about 95% of the save. Two items were plain waste:

- The census of the bytes written (`CensusStream::push`, `Census::of`) went
  through `utf8_chunks` twice for every piece: once to find a cut-off tail,
  once to count. That was about 21% of the walk. It now makes one pass with
  `str::from_utf8` (`Census::up_to_cut`). Proptests check it against the
  old `utf8_chunks` census, kept in the tests as the oracle, whole and
  streamed in random pieces. The index review's census gets faster too.
- The row parser's field list grew from empty: three reallocations a row
  for 12 columns, about 13% of the walk. It now starts with room for 16
  fields (`rows/parse.rs`, `FIELDS_HINT`).

## 2. Flaky disk-image and timing tests (should, nit)

- `DiskImage::detach` (source/tests/mod.rs) used to count the image as
  detached as soon as `hdiutil info` listed no device for it. Under load,
  `hdiutil info` may not list an image that was only just attached yet,
  so `force_detach` returned at once with the volume still mounted. That
  is the likely cause the verifier found, though it isn't reproduced. Now
  it also checks that neither mount point is a mount point any more
  (`mounted`: the folder's device differs from its parent's). Until then
  it detaches by mount point, with `-force` once the ordinary time is up.
  If the volume is still mounted when time runs out, it reports that,
  `force_detach` panics with a clear message, and the harness fails
  instead of the test seeing a wrong `Ok(())`. This covers every caller
  of `force_detach`.
- `a_file_changed_while_its_drive_was_away_is_reported` asserts its file
  is gone after the detach.
- Wall-clock upper bounds that only guard against a hang are now 5 s:
  original.rs (`NOT_BLOCKED`: three drop/status checks), share.rs:486 and
  :510, removable.rs `a_pause_stops_when_cancelled`. The real checks (try
  counts, lower bounds, test-held holds) are unchanged.

## 3. SaveAsTests restart flake (should)

`testASaveThatEndsWithARestartTakesUpTheNewReading` awaited
`model.checkOriginal()?.value`, but `checkOriginal()` returns nil while
another check is under way, such as the one the disconnect started. The
test's check then ran after the save had ended. Now:

- The test waits until no check is under way
  (`DocumentModel.isCheckingOriginalForTesting`, new), then starts its
  own and `XCTUnwrap`s it. The two happen in one main-actor turn, so
  nothing can start a check between them.
- Private notification centers keep mounts and activations elsewhere in
  the suite from starting checks.

## 4. sandbox-save-check in CI (should, nit)

- ci.yml's `check-all` job runs `just sandbox-save-check debug` right
  after "Build the scripted-run app", with a 15-minute step timeout.
  DESIGN §4.3 and the recipe's doc say so.
  - **Not yet verified on a runner.** The first push shows whether the
    VM allows it: a TCC prompt for /Volumes, or `open -a` without a
    logged-in session, would make it time out. If it does, move it to
    deep.yml or make it report-only, and record that here.
- Open Recent: the scripted build shares Leal's bundle identifier, so a
  scripted run's (deleted) files stayed in the real Leal's Open Recent.
  In the scripted build, `DocumentController` overrides
  `noteNewRecentDocumentURL` (opens and Save As both go through it), and
  `ScriptedRun.prepare` turns it off for a scripted run (checked).

## Rust notes

- **`str::from_utf8`'s error** (`Census::up_to_cut`): on bad bytes it
  returns a `Utf8Error`. `valid_up_to()` is how far the bytes were valid.
  `error_len()` is `Some(n)` for an invalid sequence of `n` bytes, or
  `None` when the bytes stop in the middle of a character. The loop
  counts the valid part, steps over the invalid bytes, and starts again
  after them. `split_at` cuts a slice in two without copying.
- **`Vec::with_capacity`** (`rows/parse.rs`) reserves room up front, so
  `push` doesn't reallocate and copy the list as it grows.
- **`MetadataExt::dev()`** (`mounted`, source/tests/mod.rs) is the
  Unix-only extension for the file's device number. A mount point is on
  another device than the folder above it.
